## What every failure has in common


Every mechanism above that "succeeds" at something real (Green-Schwarz
universality, Phase X's forbidden-representation result, Phase Y's
self-duality split) succeeds at constraining or completing the **gauge**
sector — which fields can exist, which couplings are structurally
possible, which representation is which. Every mechanism that tries to
touch the **flavor-index** freedom itself (FN charges, messenger matter,
S3 texture, RG fixed points, the spectral-action attempt, and now the
gauged-`su(3)_F` order-one condition of Z6–Z15) either adds parameters,
has zero selection power, or was honestly left incomplete. No mechanism
tested anywhere in this project's history has been shown to reduce the
count of complex Yukawa parameters below 6 (or 3). Z6–Z13 came closest —
a genuine, exactly-checked (Z13, symbolic) reduction from 6 to 1 complex
parameter, *conditional on `SU(3)_F` staying unbroken* — but Z14 found
that condition is itself the framework's own dynamically preferred
vacuum for a minimal flavon (Theorem B-3), which makes the combination a
sharper *exclusion* (predicts degenerate/zero masses) rather than
progress, and Z15 found the standard non-minimal fix (an adjoint flavon)
evades the exclusion but re-introduces at least as much freedom as it
removes once mixing is required. Z16 then supplied the "stated reason"
Z15's own writeup called for — a sextet flavon whose democratic vacuum
is *forced*, not chosen — and found that this genuinely different
mechanism lands on the *same* excluded prediction as Z14 (via a smaller,
3-generator residual `SO(3)`), reinforcing rather than escaping the
exclusion. Z17 closed off the one remaining idea in a two-flavon portal,
finding it dominated by Z15. **Z18 then tried a structurally different
representation (the decuplet) and a structurally different kind of
stabilizer (finite, not continuous) and found a third, independent
route to the same degenerate-or-zero exclusion family — requiring no
potential-minimization proof to establish, and additionally killing the
antisymmetric channel, which Z14/Z16 never directly addressed.**
