## What's new in this literature map vs. the existing backlog

`PHASE_R_HYPOTHESIS_BACKLOG.md` already had the right instinct (item 9)
but hadn't connected it to the specific dimensional recipe
(`14+52+182=248`) or to the classical Freudenthal/Tits/Baez sourcing.
That connection is this phase's actual contribution on the literature
side -- not new hypotheses, but tying an already-flagged open item to
its precise, checkable mathematical content.
