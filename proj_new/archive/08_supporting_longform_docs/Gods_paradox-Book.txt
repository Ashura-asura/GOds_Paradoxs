WHAT THIS IS (For Regular Readers)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This document is the complete record of a journey: the attempt to derive the
entire Standard Model of particle physics from pure algebra -- specifically,
from the octonions (the largest of the four division algebras) and Clifford
algebras (the mathematics of spacetime geometry).

The question being asked: "Can the Standard Model -- all its particles, charges,
forces, and symmetries -- be forced to emerge from mathematical structure alone,
without any free parameters or experimental input?"

The answer, documented phase by phase, is:

  YES -- for the content of one generation (the particles).
  YES -- for three generations (via E8 exceptional symmetry).
  NOT YET -- for the masses, mixing angles, and dynamics.

Why "God's Paradox"? Because the octonions are the most "divine" of algebras --
they are the only normed division algebra that is non-associative, making them
both beautiful and difficult. They sit at the foundation of the exceptional Lie
groups (G2, F4, E6, E7, E8) which mathematicians call "exceptional" precisely
because they are the symmetry groups of the octonions.

If the universe is, at bottom, a piece of pure mathematics, then this is where
that mathematics lives.

The framework uses a LEVEL system to denote certainty:

  L1 = Proved (mathematical theorem, no possible counterexample)
  L2 = Verified (computationally checked with explicit matrices)
  L3 = Conjecture (mechanism identified, requires further work)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  = 2*eta_{ab}*I, where eta has signature (p,q)
- Witt basis: decomposition of gamma matrices into creation/annihilation operators

PART I: THE FOUNDATION (Phases 1-4)
================================================================================

The octonions are the foundation. They are an 8-dimensional algebra over the real
numbers with the property that every non-zero element has a multiplicative inverse.
Their multiplication table is given by the Fano plane, a projective plane with 7
points and 7 lines.

The octonion structure constants are encoded in the L_i and R_i matrices --
7x7 matrices representing left and right multiplication by the imaginary
octonion units e_1 through e_7.

From these, we construct the Cl(0,7) gamma matrices as Gamma_i = sigma_1 (x) L_i,
giving a 16x16 representation. This extends to Cl(0,8) by adding Gamma_8 =
i*sigma_2 (x) I_8.

The Witt basis of Cl(0,8) gives 4 creation/annihilation pairs. The first 3 pairs
generate SU(3)_c (color), and the 4th pair generates SU(2) (weak isospin).

  Charge operator: Q = (N_1 + N_2 + N_3)/3 - N_4

where N_i = a_i^dagger a_i are the number operators for each Witt pair.
This produces charges in multiples of 1/3, exactly as in the Standard Model.

Key results:
  - SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) gauge group embedded               [L1]
  - Charge quantization Q in multiples of 1/3                 [L2]
  - sin^2 theta_W = 3/8 at GUT scale                         [L2]
  - No proton decay (no X/Y bosons in division algebras)     [L1]
