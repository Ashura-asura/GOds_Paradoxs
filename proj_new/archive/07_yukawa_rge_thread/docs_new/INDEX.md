# docs_new — Flavor Dynamics Investigation (3 sessions, chronological)

This folder holds the three sessions that followed
`SESSION_RESULT_DYNAMICAL_VACUUM_SELECTION_TASKS_1_6.md` (top level of the
repo), in order:

1. **SESSION_RESULT_QUANTUM_FLAVOR_DYNAMICS_TASKS_1_6.md**
   Coleman-Weinberg potential, fermion condensation, gap equations,
   anomalies, and a first pass at RG fixed points. Closes four of five
   routes with a structural proof; leaves the RG-fixed-point question open.

2. **SESSION_RESULT_YUKAWA_RG_FIXED_POINT_TASKS_1_7.md**
   Dedicated follow-up on the one route left open above. Derives the
   U(3) family-basis equivariance of the Yukawa beta-function system
   (30 raw parameters − 9 basis redundancy = 21 physical, matching the
   exact count of β=0 conditions). Proves, independent of that open
   question, that physical CKM/PMNS mixing cannot be fixed by any Yukawa
   RG mechanism because it depends on Higgs-sector vev ratios shown free
   in the first session of this whole series.

3. **SESSION_RESULT_2LOOP_GAUGE_YUKAWA_CLOSURE_TASKS_1_6.md**
   Attempts to close the remaining open piece with real numbers rather
   than more structural argument. Pins the gauge-loop coefficient exactly
   (C₂(16)=45/8, cited: arXiv:2403.12914 Table 9) and confirms the
   10_S/120_A/126_S structure against both the project's own
   `RESULT_TASK2_3_CG_MAPS.md` gamma-matrix computation and external
   literature (Bertolini–Frigerio–Malinsky, hep-ph/0406117). Formally
   declares the remaining Yukawa self-coupling Clebsch coefficients
   (κ, λ) unavailable in this project's current toolset, with a specific,
   checkable reason, rather than estimating them. Cites the actual
   one-loop CKM-matrix RGE (Babu, Z. Phys. C 35 (1987) 69) and shows it
   is homogeneous in the mixing matrix V with no source term — proving,
   independently of the still-open internal-alignment question, that RG
   flow cannot generate flavor mixing from a mixing-free boundary
   condition under any circumstance.

## Net status after all three sessions

- **Closed, unconditional no-go:** a complete, zero-free-parameter
  Standard Model flavor prediction (masses + CKM + PMNS + CP phases) from
  Cl(10)×E8 quantum dynamics alone, under every mechanism tested
  (Coleman-Weinberg, condensation, gap equation, anomaly-induced
  potential, RG fixed point, gauge-Yukawa asymptotic safety).
- **Open, specifically bounded:** whether the internal Yukawa-triple
  system (Y_10, Y_120, Y_126) alone has an isolated RG fixed point in its
  21-dimensional physical moduli space. Blocked on one identified,
  well-defined, uncompleted computation (SO(10) Clebsch-Gordan
  coefficients for the three-Yukawa self-energy diagrams), not on a proof
  of impossibility. Even if completed favorably, the strongest possible
  resulting claim is PARTIAL (internal alignment/hierarchy only), never
  SUCCESS, because of the two closed no-go results above.

No optimism. No pessimism. Only mathematics.
